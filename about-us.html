About CMW Heating & Cooling

CMW Heating & Cooling is an independent, family-run business providing heating, plumbing and cooling services throughout Beds, Bucks & surrounding areas. With more than 10 years' industry experience, we specialise in boiler installations, servicing, repairs, heat pumps and air conditioning systems for domestic customers.

Every job is carried out by the same experienced engineer, from quotation through to completion. We do not subcontract our work, which allows us to maintain consistently high standards of workmanship and customer care. Our reputation has been built through recommendations, repeat customers and independently verified reviews.

Whether we're carrying out an annual boiler service or installing an entirely new heating system, our aim is always the same: honest advice, quality workmanship and a professional service.
